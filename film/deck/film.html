Built on

QM
Its Memorable memory provider records procedures, not facts. That is the hook the whole product hangs on.
Memorable
Targets are named by meaning, not coordinates, so a moved button still matches next week.
GBrain
Searches the library with cited answers and says what it doesn't know yet.
BrowserSkill
Accessibility refs, not pixels, in a real logged-in browser, with a hand-off to a human for sign-ins.
