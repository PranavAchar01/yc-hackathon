How it works

Watch. Remember. Repeat.

01

Watch

Type /teach gtm and do the job the way you always do. It watches your screen and writes down every step.

Screen capture + a vision model
02

Remember

Your demonstration becomes a procedure the whole team can reuse. It lives in your own workspace, not someone else's cloud.

Procedural memory by Memorable
03

Repeat

Anyone types /gtm. Your team's agent runs the same steps, shows its work live, and waits for your OK to send.
